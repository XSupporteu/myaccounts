Learn about password requirements

Your password can be any combination of letters, numbers, and symbols. Use only ASCII-standard characters. Accents and accented characters aren't supported.

You can't use a password that:

  • Is particularly weak
    • Example: password123
  • You've used before on your account
  • Starts or ends with a blank space

Tip: Your password should be hard to guess and it shouldn't include personal info, like your phone number, initials, nickname, birthday, address that's easy to guess or find online. Avoid obvious phrases like "password" or simple sequences like "abcd" or "1234."
